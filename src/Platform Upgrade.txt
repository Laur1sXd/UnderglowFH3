FH3 Mod Platform 1.3.0 compatibility — 12 November 2026

Use the Xbox One FH3 build 1.0.125.0 with Azimuth and FH3 Mod Platform 1.3.0.
The official platform release is at:
https://github.com/Levilente/FH3_ModPlatform/releases/tag/v1.3.0

The new FH3 loader is DINPUT8.dll beside azimuth.exe. If migrating from FH3
Mod Platform 1.2.x, move the old FH3 DWrite.dll and d3dcompiler_47.dll out of
the Azimuth folder first. Keep an FH2 platform DWrite.dll if you share the
Azimuth folder with FH2. Do not replace unrelated proxy DLLs blindly.

Close Azimuth before replacing mod DLLs. Keep existing INI files, radio
folders, Car Meets data and achievement records. Use one DLL per mod:
do not keep renamed older DLLs alongside the update in fh3-mods.

The updates were built locally and passed automated native-image startup,
hook-sharing and regression tests. No new interactive driving/visual test
of these v1.3.0 builds has been performed. The main game and saves were not
modified. No release has been published to GitHub.
